A voice that transcends boundaries. A philosophy that resonates across musical worlds.

Innovative soprano Noriko Yakushiji moves freely across musical genres, rooted in classical vocal tradition while exploring contemporary music, Noh chant (Utai) and pop. Her artistic practice embraces diverse musical languages, opening up new possibilities for vocal expression.

She has performed world premieres and Japanese premieres of works by numerous composers, including Henri Pousseur, Salvatore Sciarrino, Claude Ledoux, Silvano Bussotti, Yu Kuwabara, Miharu Ogura, Hidehiko Hinohara, Yoriaki Matsudaira and Motoharu Kawashima. Most recently, she took part in the Japanese premiere of Sciarrino’s Una lettera e sei canti in Tokyo, receiving praise from the composer himself. A live recording of the performance was released by NEOS Records on the album Ai limiti della notte – Sciarrino in Tokyo 2024.

She has also performed in classical opera productions featuring works by composers such as Henry Purcell, Claudio Monteverdi and Maurice Ravel in Belgium and the UK. In contemporary opera, she has created roles in several world premieres, including Alto in Yoriaki Matsudaira’s The Provocators, the title role in Miharu Ogura’s mono-opera Call, Rito in Riles Walsh’s The East Complex, and a role in Koji Takahashi’s Amidst Dust and Fractured Voices.

In 2024, she appeared in B→C (Bach to Contemporary), a recital series organised by Tokyo Opera City Foundation. Her programme explored Noh vocal techniques and melismatic singing, receiving acclaim from several contemporary music publications.

She has appeared at numerous international festivals, including Ars Musica in Belgium, Belgian Music Days, Suntory Hall Summer Festival in Japan, the Sirga International Contemporary Music Festival in Spain, and Tracking Tilburg in the Netherlands. At Festival Vlaanderen Lunalia 2025, she collaborated with the Centre for New Music MATRIX on Stripsody and performed in the festival’s opening concert.

Her performances have also been broadcast on VRT Radio Klara in Belgium and NHK-FM in Japan.

She was named a Laureate and Finalist of Les Nouveaux Talents de l’Art Lyrique d’Ans in 2014 and Le Concours Bell’Arte in 2017, both in Belgium. In 2018, she was awarded Third Prize at the Kōgaku International Contemporary Music Competition in Tokyo, Japan.

Alongside her contemporary music activities, she has a strong interest in pop culture. In 2025 and 2026, she took part in a European tour of Studio Ghibli and anime song concerts, performing at major venues across Belgium and the Netherlands, including the Théâtre royal de Mons (Royal Theatre of Mons), Muziekgebouw Eindhoven and AFAS Circustheater in The Hague.

Together with Pianist and electronic musician Quentin Meurisse, she founded Uguisu-labo, a duo exploring contemporary music through voice and live electronics for audiences of all ages, from babies to adults. The duo has appeared at Festival Mosaïques in Brussels.

She has also collaborated with Uta × Koto, an ensemble of contemporary voice, koto and clarinet founded by composer Sehyon Jo in 2018. The project explored new forms of expression at the intersection of contemporary and classical music, and Western and Eastern musical traditions.

After graduating from Tokyo University of the Arts, she continued her studies in Belgium with a grant from Wallonie-Bruxelles International. Under the guidance of Marianne Pousseur, she completed her Master’s degree at the Royal Conservatory of Brussels with a scholarship from the Nomura Foundation. She subsequently completed the Advanced Master’s programme in Contemporary Music at the Ictus / Spectra Academy of the Royal Conservatoire of Ghent, under the supervision of Filip Rathé.

She currently studies Utai (Noh chant) under Tomotaka Sekine, a performer of the Kanze school of Noh.
